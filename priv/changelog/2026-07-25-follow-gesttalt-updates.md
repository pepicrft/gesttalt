Gesttalt now has a public home for user-facing product updates. Each entry will explain what changed and why it matters, so you can understand new capabilities without tracing internal development work.

You can follow along through [Really Simple Syndication](https://www.rssboard.org/rss-specification) or [Atom](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4287) in the feed reader you already use.
