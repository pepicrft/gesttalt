  summary: "Publications can now start with Paper, Ledger, Darkroom, or Field Notes before making their own theme changes."

The Theme dashboard now offers four built-in designs: Paper, Ledger, Darkroom, and Field Notes. Choose one to give a publication a clear starting point for writing and photography. The publishing dashboard also adopts the selected theme’s colors, typography, and semantic control colors, including readable action-control contrast, so the working environment reflects the publication. Changing a theme keeps your current position on the page, and the chooser now has clear separation from the editing workflow below it. The photography feed aligns with its publishing form. The public marketing pages retain their centered reading layout when a dashboard theme is active.

Built-in themes stay connected to future improvements until you or an agent customize their templates, styles, or variables. Selecting a built-in theme replaces an existing custom theme, so it provides a deliberate way to return to an application-maintained design.
