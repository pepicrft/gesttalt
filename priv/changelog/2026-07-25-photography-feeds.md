  title: "Publish a photography feed",
  summary: "Gesttalt publications can now share a chronological photo feed from the dashboard, an application, or an agent."
}
---
Every publication now has a dedicated photography feed at `/photography`. It keeps visual work alongside long-form writing without forcing each photograph into an article.

Photographs can begin as private drafts or be published immediately. Each entry carries alternative text, an optional caption, and its publication date. The same workflow is available in the dashboard, through the application programming interface, and through the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/), so people and agents can use one shared publishing model.

The built-in Paper theme includes a calm, single-column feed inspired by familiar social photography layouts while leaving out engagement counters and other noise. Its Liquid template and styles remain fully editable through live theme sessions.
