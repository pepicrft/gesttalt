%{
  title: "Accounts are limited to invited addresses",
  summary: "Only approved email addresses can create an account, sign in, or connect tools."
}
---

Gesttalt now accepts accounts only for approved email addresses. Other addresses cannot register, receive sign-in links, sign in with a password, or use access tokens for the application programming interface and the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server. Sessions and tokens that belong to other addresses stop working.
